Fig. 2. Schematic of energy yield modelling for multi-junction solar modules. The power generation of the tilted multi-junction solar module varies with the sun’s specular and diﬀuse irradiance, which is linked to the location, time, orientation of the solar module and the cloud coverage. For diﬀerent architectures (monofacial or bifacial) and module installations (albedo), additional contributions of incident light to the generated current need to be considered.
