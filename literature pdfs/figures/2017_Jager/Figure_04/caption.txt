Fig. 4. Estimating the tandem cell eﬃciency of regular (dashed lines) and inverted (solid lines) device architectures as a function of the perovskite bandgap. The following parameters are shown: the open-circuit voltage (Voc), being similar for both architectures, the optimized absorber thickness of the perovskite top-cell as well as the maximum achievable current density Jph, and the tandem-cell power conversion eﬃciency (PCE). For the PCE calculation a FF of 81% and a silicon Voc of 709 mV in tandem geometry was assumed. The perovskite (n, k) data for the diﬀerent bandgaps was obtained by blue-shifting the original data.
