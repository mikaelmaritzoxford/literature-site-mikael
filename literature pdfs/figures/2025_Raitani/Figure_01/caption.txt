Figure 1. Location-agnostic, idealized phase map illustrating perform- ance−stability trade-offs in 2-T Perovskite-Silicon tandem solar cells. (a) Efficiency limits for 2-T tandem cells (at 300 K) for different top cell bandgaps (EgT) as per Shockley−Queisser (SQ) analysis. The insets show a schematic of P−Si 2-T tandem solar cell and its equivalent circuit model. (b) Phase map of the ratio of degradation rates (d2/d1) at which a device with η2 has the same LCOE as a device with η1 (d1 = 0.03, r = 0.065, and N = 25 years; other parameters are assumed to be the same).
