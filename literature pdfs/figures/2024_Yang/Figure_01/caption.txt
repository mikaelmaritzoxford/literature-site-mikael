Fig. 1. Schematic diagram of the monolithic perovskite-Si tandem devices utilized for optical and electric simulations. (a)- (c) are defined as “textured back-side-only” silicon, “textured double-side” silicon with “textured back-side” perovskite, and “textured double-side” silicon with “textured double-side” perovskite, respectively. The film thicknesses of electrodes and antireflective coating (AR-LiF) employed in the simulations are also indicated. It should be noted that the thickness of the perovskite films varies depending on the optimization of matched current densities.
