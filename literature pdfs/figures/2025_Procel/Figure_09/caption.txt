Fig. 9. Modelled hourly spectra assigned to the corresponding time slots, where each row represents the month of the year, and each column represents the hour of the day. The number in each cell indicates the number of unique spectra modelled by SMARTS at the each (month, hour) pair in Delft, the Netherlands. 4497 spectral irradiances were modelled for Delft at each hour of each month of the year. The white-colored cells bear no information, as the sun disk was below the horizon, so no spectral irradiance could be extracted.
