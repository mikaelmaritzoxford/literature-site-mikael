Fig. 2. Flowchart of Stage 5 (Semiconductor Physics). Among the input parameters we enlist permittivity (ε), temperature (T), mobility (μ), electron affinity (χ), band gap (Eg), tunneling mass of electrons and holes (mt(•)), carriers’ density or doping concentration (N(•)), density of states (DOS). The input parameters are fed to various physical models and are coupled with the semiconductor equations: the Poisson equation and the continuity equation, where J(•) stands for the current density of electron or holes. Such equations are solved consistently with the Fermi-Dirac statistics, where E, EF and kB are energy, Fermi energy, and Boltzmann constant, respectively. The results are represented as band diagrams, spectral response spectra, current density – voltage (J–V) curve, and losses breakdown. This figure is adapted from Ref. [38].
