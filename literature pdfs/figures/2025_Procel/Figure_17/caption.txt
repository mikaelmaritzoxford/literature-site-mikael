Fig. 17. Simulated annual energy yield improvement (ΔEY) at the DC level for various PV module archetypes, relative to the M12 monofacial reference module. Results are shown for two European locations: Delft (northern Europe, solid bars) and Catania (southern Europe, patterned bars). The analysis includes M2 modules with monofacial, bifacial, and tandem (2J) configurations featuring different interface morphologies (F/F in blue, F/T in red, T/T in yellow, T/T Rin green), as well as G12-type modules with T/T tandem cells.
