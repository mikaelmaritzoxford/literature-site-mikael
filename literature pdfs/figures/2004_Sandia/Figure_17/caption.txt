Figure 17. Photo of a wiring ‘combiner box’ housing the terminations for 17 high-voltage module-strings in a large array. In this case, each module string had twelve 150-Wp crystalline silicon modules connected in series. It is possible to ‘translate’ an accurate (±1%) module-string Voc measurement to the value at the Standard Reporting Condition with a resulting uncertainty less than ±2%. This translation is accomplished by simultaneously recording reasonably accurate (±10%) values for the effective solar irradiance and for module temperature (±3°C) and then applying Equation (15).
