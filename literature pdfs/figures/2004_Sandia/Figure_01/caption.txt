Basic Equations The following equations define the model used by the Solar Technologies Department at Sandia for analyzing and modeling the performance of photovoltaic modules. The equations describe the electrical performance for individual photovoltaic modules, and can be scaled for any series or parallel combination of modules in an array. The same equations apply equally well for individual cells, for individual modules, for large arrays of modules, and for both flat-plate and concentrator modules.
