Fig. 3. Variation of radiative recombination in a perovskite solar cell under different conditions. (a) Comparison of energy yield (EY) calculated for constant and power-law varying radiative recombination with excitation intensity (k = 0.6) in a solar cell with a typical 150 μm silicon thickness. (b) Optimization of perovskite thickness under two conditions—with and without luminescent coupling (LC) effects, while the thickness of the bottom cell is fixed at 250 μm and the bandgap of perovskite is 1.64 eV. Optimization of both absorber layers with variations in radiative recombination relative to excitation intensity, shown with (c) and without (d) LC effects.
