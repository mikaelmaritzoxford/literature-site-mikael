Fig. 2. Influence of radiative recombination on energy yield (EY) and luminescent coupling (LC) effects. (a) Comparison of EY values derived from optimizing the thickness of the top subcell (800 nm) in a 100 μm silicon-based tandem structure at different external electroluminescence quantum efficiencies (ELQE) of 8.4 % and 25 %, with and without LC effects.
