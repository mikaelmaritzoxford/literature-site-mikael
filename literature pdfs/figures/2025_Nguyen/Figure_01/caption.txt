Fig. 1. Effects of luminescent coupling (LC) on the optimization of a monolithic perovskite/silicon tandem structures based on energy yield (EY) cal­ culations from Typical Meteorological Year (TMY) data in Daggett, USA. (a) Variation in EY at different thicknesses of the top subcell (300–800 nm) and bottom subcell (100–450 μm) in a monolithic perovskite/silicon tandem structure, with and without LC effects, at an external electroluminescence quantum efficiency of 25 % and a perovskite bandgap of 1.64 eV. (b–c) Contour plots showing thickness optimization of EY with and without LC effects. The stars indicate the thickness optimization of each subcell needed to reach 98 % of the maximum EY with the thinnest silicon bottom cell.
