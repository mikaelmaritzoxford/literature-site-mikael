## Experimental procedures

<u>Silicon heterojunction bottom cell fabrication</u>

Float-zone Si wafers (Topsil, PV-FZ, 1-5 Ω.cm) with thicknesses of 280 ±20 µm are used for silicon heterojunction bottom cell fabrication. Pyramids on both sides with heights of less than 1µm are obtained with an alkaline solution. After texturing the wafers go through RCA cleaning and a final hydrofluoric acid bath before transferring to the plasma-enhanced chemical vapour deposition (PECVD, Indeotec Octopus II). Intrinsic, doped amorphous silicon, and nano-crystalline silicon are deposited at 200°C. 10nm of IZO is sputtered by physical vapour deposition (PVD) and serves as a recombination junction on top of the nc- Si:H(n). 150nm of ITO and 250nm of Ag are sputtered on the rear side.

<u>Perovskite top cell fabrication</u>

The silicon bottom cells are subjected to a 10-minute UV-Ozone treatment before being transferred to an N₂-filled glovebox. 2PACz (1 mg/ml, ethanol) is spin-coated at 5000 rpm for 30 s, followed by drying at 100°C for 8 minutes. The triple cation perovskite solution Cs0.05FA0.8MA0.15Pb(I0.75Br0.25)3 (1.7 M, DMF:DMSO 4:1 v/v) is prepared by dissolving a mixture of FAI, MABr (Greatcell Solar Materials), CsI (Sigma Aldrich), PbI₂, and PbBr₂ (Alfa Aesar). The bandgap of the perovkite is 1.68eV. This perovskite precursor solution is spin-coated on the bottom cells using a two-step spin-coating process: initially spinning at 2,000 rpm and then at 7,000 rpm. During the acceleration between the two steps, chlorobenzene is deposited as a solvent quencher. The films are then annealed in the glovebox at 100 °C for 15 min. 20 nm of C₆₀ (nano-C) is then thermally evaporated (Angstrom EvoVac) on top of the perovskite. A 20-nm- thick layer of tin oxide was deposited by atomic layer deposition, using tetrakis(dimethylamino)tin and H₂O as the precursors with N₂ as the gas carrier. As the top electrode, 70 nm of IZO is sputtered in an Angstrom –6 EvoVac sputtering system (base pressure <1 × 10 Torr). To functionally contact the transparent −6 electrode, 350 nm of silver contacts were thermally evaporated (Angstrom EvoVac) (base pressure 1 × 10 Torr). Finally, a 100 nm MgF₂ antireflective coating was deposited onto the finished device by thermal evaporation. The device area of the silicon-perovskite tandem is 1 cm².

### <u>Solar cell characterization and outdoor testing</u>

J-V measurements are performed under an LED-based solar simulator (Wavelabs Sinus 220). The intensities of the blue LED channels are modified to obtain different spectra. The resulting spectral irradiances are measured by the Wavelabs internal spectrophotometer. The unencapsulated devices are measured using an aperture area and our homemade program controlling a Keithley 2400 series SourceMeter. The device temperature is controlled using a vacuum stage with Peltier elements. The EQE is performed using the PV-tools LOANA that includes a temperature-controlled stage.

<u>Encapsulation</u>

Solar ribbons are connected to the contact of the tandem with silver paste to extend the electrodes. The device is laminated between 2 layers of encapsulant (TPU) and 2 glasses. Butyl rubber serves as an edge sealant to avoid moisture ingress from the sides. The lamination (Ecolam5, Ecoprogetti) temperature is set at 120°C for 25mn.
