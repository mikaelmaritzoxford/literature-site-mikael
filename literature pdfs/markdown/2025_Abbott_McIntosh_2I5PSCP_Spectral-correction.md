CONCLUSION “Full-stack” simulation and analyses—such as that presented in this study—connects the fields of cell, module and system research. This is particularly valuable for 2TT technologies, much more so than c-Si technologies, because of their sensitivity to spectrum. Full-stack simulation permits cell and module technologists to design and test devices for the ultimate operating conditions, and it assists system engineers in reconfiguring system components for the modules of the future.
